Fiber Optic Networks

Cuvette Engineering (U) Ltd designs, builds, and maintains high-performance fiber optic networks that support the growing demand for reliable, high-speed connectivity. Our fiber solutions cover the full project lifecycle — from planning and rollout to testing, commissioning, and long-term operational support.

What We Deliver

  • FTTH / FTTx Network Design & Implementation
  • GIS-Based Route Surveys, Ducting & Trenching
  • Precision Fusion Splicing & Fiber Termination
  • OTDR, Power Meter & Link Budget Testing
  • Backbone & Metro Fiber Network Deployment
  • Preventive Maintenance & Fault Restoration

Engineering Standards & Quality Assurance

All fiber optic works are executed in compliance with international standards including ITU-T, IEC, IEEE, and FOA best practices. We use advanced fusion splicing equipment, OTDR analyzers, and certified testing tools to ensure minimal signal loss, optimal performance, and long-term network stability.

Our Strength

  • FOA-trained and certified fiber optic engineers
  • Advanced splicing, testing & commissioning equipment
  • Experience working with ISPs and telecom operators
  • Detailed documentation and as-built reports
