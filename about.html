Who We Are

Cuvette Engineering (U) Ltd is a premier construction and infrastructure development firm dedicated to delivering high-quality, sustainable, and innovative building solutions. Founded in 2019, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in Uganda's construction industry, known for our professionalism, reliability, and technical expertise.

With a team of highly qualified engineers, architects, project managers, and skilled laborers, we specialize in a broad range of services—from residential and commercial construction to infrastructure projects,telecom engineering services, renovations, maintenance, CCTV/security systems, and electro-mechanical equipment. We pride ourselves on completing projects on time, within budget, and to the highest standards of quality.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, cost-effective, and sustainable construction solutions that exceed our clients' expectations. We aim to foster long-term relationships through transparency, safety, and unparalleled quality in all our projects.

Our Vision

To be a leader in the construction industry, recognized for delivering exceptional projects that shape sustainable communities and drive economic growth.
